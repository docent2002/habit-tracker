## 4. AIFC LEGAL STRUCTURE

Private Company Limited by Shares is the **correct vehicle**. A Swiss GmbH may be sole shareholder;
100 % corporate ownership is permitted.

**Restricted Scope Company is NOT recommended** — AFSA registration is currently suspended, and it
would force the suffix **"Restricted Ltd."**, which is actively unhelpful in UN/MDB procurement.

### The Route A / Route B fork — the central drafting decision

R01's remedy ("adopt Schedule 5 wholesale") and R04's remedy ("delete the IIN and residency
clauses") are **mutually exclusive**: Schedule 5 Art. 17 *is* the IIN requirement and Art. 18.2 *is*
the residency requirement.

| | **Route A — adopt Schedule 5 entire** | **Route B — bespoke, clauses deleted** |
|---|---|---|
| s.13(5)/s.14(5)/COR 2.2.2 | Disapplied | All engaged |
| Director IIN | **Required** (Art. 17 adopted) | **Not required** |
| CEO residency | Adopted; dormant only if no CEO | Deleted |
| Registrar scrutiny | Minimal | Clause-by-clause on first filing |
| Inherited defects | Adopts six of AFSA's own Schedule 5 errors | May be fixed, but each fix is a deviation |

> **The current package is the worst cell in the matrix: Route B's exposure with Route A's content.**
> It is bespoke (drops Sch. 5 Art. 4(b), rewrites Art. 6, adds Art. 31), so it carries every
> COR 2.2.2 and s.14(5) obligation — while retaining precisely the two Schedule 5 clauses that
> disqualify the intended officer.

**Recommendation:** if the Registrar confirms the CEO is optional **and** an IIN is being obtained
anyway (needed for bank, EDS and BIN regardless), take **Route A**. If the CEO is mandatory,
Route A is impossible and **Route B is forced** — done properly, with COR 2.2.2(i) and (r) added.

## 5. AFSA PERIMETER CONCLUSION

**No AFSA licence is required. The business is a Non-Regulated Business.**

"Providing Consulting Services" is a listed Ancillary Service (GEN Sch. 2 para 4), but the
Consultancy Guidance para 5 confines the regime to consultancy *"provided in relation to Financial
Services"*, across four species. Sustainability advisory to UN agencies and MDBs reaches none of them.

**Note the asymmetry:** the client is outside the **licence** (a *subject-matter* test) and outside
the **tax exemption** (a *counterparty* test). Different tests — which is why the licensing good news
does not rescue the tax position.

### Three perimeter exposures to manage

| Exposure | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| **Environmental Instruments are Investments** — carbon credits, allowances, RECs (GLO). Instrument-specific carbon advice is *Advising on Investments*. AIX runs a live Carbon Platform | **The likeliest accidental breach** for a sustainability practice — more so than green bonds |
| **The risk is *Arranging*, not *Advising*** | No "capacity as investor" limit; catches introductions producing **no** transaction |
| **"Structuring" is not a safe harbour** | PERG expressly includes structuring within Advising on Investments |

### A commercial opening
MAR 1.10.4 requires ESG-bond issuers to appoint external review providers but **licenses no one to
be one**. Second-party opinions, taxonomy alignment and post-issuance verification for green,
sustainability-linked and sukuk issues can be provided **licence-free**. This is squarely the
client's expertise and may be a better rationale for the structure than the tax argument.

## 7. KAZAKHSTAN IMPLEMENTATION CONCLUSION

**The critical path is not what the file assumed.**

AFSA's current Guidance para 3.3 states Director eligibility as *"at least 18 years old and free of
disqualifying factors"* — **no IIN**. The CEO *"must have a Kazakhstani IIN"*. Nothing in ComReg
Parts 4 or 7 requires a Director to hold an IIN. The requirement exists in exactly one place:
**Schedule 5 Art. 17** — a template default the company may decline (s.14(4): "the whole **or any
part**").

> **The IIN gate is therefore self-inflicted, not a requirement of AIFC law.** It must not be
> presented to the client as immovable. It is either accepted deliberately (Route A) or deleted
> (Route B).

An IIN will still be needed in practice for the **bank account, the e-signature (EDS) and the entity
BIN** — so obtaining one is sensible regardless, but it does **not** gate the Registrar filing.

**No Kazakhstan residency requirement** exists for an AIFC director or CEO in law. AIFC law imposes
residency where it means to (the MLRO must be ordinarily resident) — a telling contrast.

**Two PoA regimes, not one:** AFSA accepts English-only without apostille; notaries, the State
Revenue Committee and banks require apostille plus notarised RU/KZ translation. **Two instruments
are needed.**

## 8. TAX CONCLUSION

> **On the intended client base, the AIFC tax exemption is not obtained. Expect 20 % Kazakhstan CIT
> on worldwide profit and 16 % VAT above the ~KZT 41.48m threshold — the ordinary mainland regime,
> at AIFC cost.**

The exemption has two limbs. Sustainability advisory to UN/MDBs fails both:
- **Financial-services limb** (Constitutional Statute Art. 6(3)) — advisory is not among the listed
  services.
- **Ancillary/consulting limb** — the *service type* qualifies, but the exemption reaches consulting
  supplied **to AIFC Bodies and AIFC Participants providing financial services**. UN agencies and
  MDBs are neither.

**Blunt commercial consequence:** on the tax metric alone, **doing this work from Zug is cheaper than
doing it from Astana** — the AIFC company adds a 20 % CIT layer the Swiss parent would not suffer on
the same fees, plus KZ payroll charges, audit, substance cost and Substantial Presence reporting.

### Two important qualifications the Red Team established

1. **R05's reasoning is wrong even though its conclusion holds.** Schedule 1 to the joint List is
   *"A Regulated Activity and Market Activity listed in GEN"* — so **Advising on Investments is an
   exempt financial service with NO counterparty test.** A route to exemption therefore exists that
   the tax review missed. It requires becoming licensed — a different business, with capital and
   compliance cost — but it exists.
2. **Consequently, R02's proposed constitutional covenant** *("The Company does not provide, and
   shall not provide, any of its services in relation to Financial Services")* **must NOT be
   adopted.** It would permanently foreclose that route in the company's own constitution.
   Describe the business accurately; do not covenant against a future.

**No green/ESG-specific exemption exists.** The Green Finance Centre is itself a licensed consulting
ASP — instructive. Its value here is credibility and partnership, not tax.

subscription clause" blocker is **substantially cured by statute** (s.13(4)(d), s.16(2)(a), s.41(1))
and is downgraded to MEDIUM.

**What genuinely needs work:**
- The **structure chart is unsigned, undated and KYC-incomplete** — yet it is the only document in
  the package stating any ownership percentage. It never uses the word "UBO", states no
  jurisdictions, legal forms or registration numbers.
- A **c/o Treuhand registered address** attracts additional bank scrutiny; mitigate with the register
  extract and a clear substance narrative.
- The PoA's pre-incorporation bank powers are governed by **s.31** (pre-incorporation contracts),
  **not nullity** — making the two attorneys **personally liable** on them. That is a real exposure
  to the attorneys and a reason to split the instrument.
