## 3. Detailed findings

### D01-01 — The chart never uses the word "UBO" and makes no UBO assertion
**Severity: HIGH · FINDING TYPE: FACT + LEGAL REQUIREMENT**

Evidence: full text of the slide (§1) — no occurrence of "UBO" / "Ultimate Beneficial Owner".

AIFC Companies Regulations **Part 14-1 (Ultimate Beneficial Owners)** requires a Relevant Person
to keep a **Beneficial Ownership Register** recording **"UBO Details" in respect of each of its
Ultimate Beneficial Owners**, and to record changes within **14 days** of becoming aware of them.
The UBO test captures a person who directly or indirectly owns or controls **at least 25%** of
voting rights, or who can appoint or remove a majority of the Directors, or who otherwise
exercises significant control or influence. **[T1-snippet — AIFC Companies Regulations Part 14-1;
AFSA *AIFC Guidance on Ultimate Beneficial Owner*]**

A 100%/100% chain plainly satisfies that test for Alexander Wiese. But the chart *states an
ownership fact*; it does not *make the UBO declaration*. Those are different instruments and the
Registrar and any bank will require the second. **This chart cannot be filed as a UBO declaration.**

### D01-02 — No natural-person identifiers for the UBO
**Severity: HIGH · FINDING TYPE: LEGAL REQUIREMENT**

The slide gives a name only. "UBO Details" under Part 14-1 are understood to comprise full name,
date of birth, nationality, residential address, identification-document type and number, the
date on which the person became a UBO, and the **nature and extent** of the interest held.
**[T1-snippet — Part 14-1; confirm the exact enumerated list against the published text.]**
None of these is present. Note also the established fact that Alexander Wiese is a **German
national resident in Switzerland** with **no Kazakhstan IIN** — a combination that AFSA and any
Kazakh bank will want spelled out, not inferred.

### D01-03 — No jurisdictions or registration numbers
**Severity: MEDIUM · FINDING TYPE: PRACTICAL REQUIREMENT**

The GmbH's registration number **CHE-212.321.848** and its registered address
**c/o Guthirt Treuhand, Ibelweg 18a, 6300 Zug** are known and appear in the Power of Attorney
[PoA line 7] — but not on the chart. A structure chart that omits the registered number of the
only *existing* entity in the chain forces every reviewer to cross-refer to another document to
identify the company. This is the single cheapest defect to fix.

### D01-04 — Percentages are unqualified
**Severity: MEDIUM · FINDING TYPE: LEGAL INTERPRETATION**

`100 %` is ambiguous as between share/quota capital and voting rights. For the Swiss GmbH the
relevant unit is **Stammanteile** (quotas), not shares; for the AIFC company it will be
**ordinary shares**. Under Part 14-1 the UBO test is expressed in terms of **voting rights** and
**control**, so a chart that reports only capital percentages does not, on its face, answer the
statutory question. Where ownership and voting coincide — as they will here — say so expressly
("100% of the quotas, carrying 100% of the voting rights").

### D01-05 — No negative confirmation
**Severity: HIGH (for KYC) · FINDING TYPE: PRACTICAL REQUIREMENT**

The chart is silent on the points every compliance reviewer actually tests: that there are **no
other shareholders**, **no nominee or bare-trustee arrangements**, **no trust, foundation or
fiduciary layer**, **no shareholders' agreement, option, pledge or usufruct** affecting the
quotas, and **no other person exercising control**. A chart proves nothing by omission; banks
require the statement.

### D01-06 — "(Authorised Signatory)" is an undefined third role label
**Severity: MEDIUM · FINDING TYPE: FACT**

Across the package Alexander Wiese is described as:

| Document | Line | Title used |
|---|---|---|
| Corporate Structure chart | run 8 | **Authorised Signatory** |
| Power of Attorney | line 7 | **Managing Director** of the GmbH |
| Power of Attorney (RU) | line 8 | **Директор** (drops "Managing") |
| Resolution, body | line 12 | **Director** of the GmbH |
| Resolution, signature block | line 43 | **Chief Executive Officer** (for the GmbH) |
| Resolution, resolved clauses | lines 21, 23, 26 | **CEO / Director / Authorised Signatory** of Eurasia Ltd. |
| AoA signature block | line 602 | **Chief Executive Officer** (for the GmbH) |

Five distinct titles for one man across four documents. On the chart the label is worst, because
it does not even name the entity he signs for. A Swiss GmbH's representative body is the
**Geschäftsführer**; "Chief Executive Officer" and "Authorised Signatory" are not Swiss
corporate-law offices. The signature block should read, in terms:
*"Alexander Wiese, Managing Director (Geschäftsführer), for and on behalf of Wiese Advisory
GmbH, CHE-212.321.848"*, and be consistent with the Swiss commercial-register entry.

### D01-07 — Eurasia Ltd. is not marked as prospective
**Severity: MEDIUM · FINDING TYPE: PRACTICAL REQUIREMENT**

`Wiese Advisory Eurasia Ltd.` is drawn as an existing entity. It does not exist. A chart submitted
with an incorporation application should mark it **"(in incorporation / proposed)"**. This is not
pedantry: an undated chart showing a non-existent company as existing is precisely the kind of
document a compliance officer flags.

### D01-08 — Supplied unsigned and undated
**Severity: MEDIUM · FINDING TYPE: FACT**

Runs 9 and 10 are empty placeholders. An unexecuted structure chart has no evidential value.
Note that the chart, unlike the Power of Attorney, at least **provides a date field** — the PoA's
signature lines [PoA lines 93, 97] have none (see D02).

### D01-09 — No "as at" date and no source attribution
**Severity: LOW · FINDING TYPE: PRACTICAL REQUIREMENT**

Ownership statements are time-sensitive. The chart should carry an **"Ownership and control
structure as at [date]"** caption distinct from the signature date, so that a bank refreshing KYC
in 18 months knows what period the statement covered. The file was authored by the Kazakh
service provider (`Diana D`), not by the client whose ownership it asserts — the signature is
what converts it into the client's representation, which makes D01-08 the binding defect.

### D01-10 — The chart is drawn correctly on the substance
**Severity: — · FINDING TYPE: FACT (positive)**

For completeness: the *substance* of the chart matches the established facts. Alexander Wiese is
confirmed sole 100% UBO of the GmbH; the GmbH is intended to be sole shareholder of the AIFC
company [Resolution line 18; PoA line 29 "the Sole Shareholder's Resolution"]. There is **no
substantive error** in the chain. Every finding above is one of *insufficiency*, not inaccuracy.
The chart is a good skeleton that has not been dressed.

## 4. Evidence required to substantiate the two ownership links

### 4.1 Link 1 — Alexander Wiese owns 100% of Wiese Advisory GmbH

This is the **easy** link, and the package does not yet exploit how easy it is.

**FINDING TYPE: LEGAL REQUIREMENT.** Under **Swiss Code of Obligations art. 791**, the members
of a GmbH must be entered in the **commercial register** by name and domicile, together with the
number and nominal value of their quotas (*Stammanteile*). Unlike a Swiss AG, a GmbH's
quotaholders are therefore a matter of **public record**, freely searchable via the federal
register index (Zefix) and obtainable as a certified extract from the Commercial Register of the
Canton of Zug. **[T1-snippet — CO art. 791; confirm against the published article text.]**

| # | Evidence item | Purpose | Priority |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Certified extract from the Commercial Register of the Canton of Zug** for CHE-212.321.848, dated within ~3 months, **apostilled** | Primary proof of (i) the GmbH's existence and good standing, (ii) Alexander Wiese's 100% quotaholding, (iii) **his signature authority** — sole (*Einzelunterschrift*) or collective (*Kollektivunterschrift zu zweien*) | **Essential** |
| 2 | Current **Statuten** (articles) of the GmbH, certified | Confirms no special quota classes, no transfer restrictions, no third-party consent rights | Essential |
| 3 | The GmbH's **Anteilbuch** (quota register) | Corroborates the register extract at company level | Recommended |
| 4 | The GmbH's **register of beneficial owners** (CO arts. 697j–697l / 790a) | Swiss-law UBO record; directly answers the beneficial-ownership question | Recommended |
| 5 | Alexander Wiese's **passport** (certified copy) + **proof of residential address** in Switzerland (utility bill / Wohnsitzbestätigung), plus a certified copy of his Swiss residence permit | Identity and residency of the UBO | Essential |
| 6 | **Signed UBO declaration** in AFSA and bank format | Converts the chart into a declaration | Essential |
| 7 | Written confirmation of **no nominee, trust or fiduciary arrangement** over the quotas | Closes D01-05 | Essential |

Note the significance of item 1 for the whole package: if the Zug register shows Alexander Wiese
with only **collective** signature authority, the Power of Attorney, the Resolution and the
Articles — each bearing a single signature — are all **void as against the GmbH**. This must be
checked before anything is executed. See D02-14.

## 5. Sufficiency, judged separately for the three purposes

### (a) AIFC incorporation — **INSUFFICIENT, but close; not the binding constraint**

**FINDING TYPE: LEGAL INTERPRETATION.**

The structure chart is a *supporting* document in an AIFC incorporation application, not the
operative one. The operative documents are the application form, the Articles, the shareholder
resolution and the shareholder/director/UBO information — all of which the Power of Attorney
correctly identifies as separate filings [PoA line 29]. AFSA's registration process expects a
structure chart to orient the reviewer; the substance is proved by the Swiss register extract and
the identity documents.

Verdict: with the additions in §6 (jurisdictions, registration number, UBO label, date,
signature), the chart would be **fit for purpose** for incorporation. It is not the reason this
filing is blocked — the CEO-residency conflict, the capital arithmetic and the resolution defects
are (see D03, D04).

`UNCONFIRMED`: whether AFSA's current registration form *mandates* a structure chart, and in what
format. Confirming step: AFSA *Guidance on the Registration Process in the AIFC* and the
Registrar's prescribed incorporation form — both of which this environment could not retrieve.

### (b) UBO filing — **INSUFFICIENT**

**FINDING TYPE: LEGAL REQUIREMENT.**

The chart fails on its face. Part 14-1 requires **UBO Details** — identity particulars and the
nature and extent of the interest — recorded in a **Beneficial Ownership Register**, with a
14-day change-notification duty, and (for a Private Company) an available election for the
Registrar to hold that information instead. **[T1-snippet — Part 14-1.]** A slide bearing a name
and a percentage supplies none of that, and does not even use the term.

What is needed instead: a **UBO declaration** in the Registrar's form, backed by the passport,
proof of address and the Zug register extract, and the establishment of the company's own
Beneficial Ownership Register on incorporation. The chart is an *annex* to that, not a substitute.

### (c) Banking KYC — **CLEARLY INSUFFICIENT**

**FINDING TYPE: PRACTICAL REQUIREMENT.**

Second-tier Kazakh banks (and any correspondent bank behind them) will not open an account for a
newly-incorporated AIFC company owned by a foreign holding company on the strength of an unsigned
slide. A bank onboarding pack for this structure will require, at minimum:

- a **signed and dated** ownership *and control* chart, traced to natural persons, on the client's
  letterhead;
- certified/apostilled Swiss corporate documents (§4.1 items 1–2);
- UBO identification documents and the bank's own UBO declaration form;
- **source of wealth** of the UBO and **source of funds** for the initial capital — note that the
  intended business (sustainability advisory to UN agencies and MDBs) is credible and
  well-documented, and the client should lead with it;
- a description of expected turnover, counterparties and payment corridors;
- confirmation of tax residence / self-certification (CRS), and PEP and sanctions screening
  declarations;
- resolution of the **authorised-signatory** question — and here the package works against
  itself: the PoA correctly withholds all account-operating powers from the attorneys
  [PoA lines 66–73], but the chart's bare `(Authorised Signatory)` label invites exactly the
  confusion the PoA was careful to avoid.

Two structural points compound this. First, the **objects clause** of the Articles describes the
business as advisory *"in the fields of finance"* [AoA line 114] while the actual business is
sustainability advisory — a mismatch a bank's compliance function will query, and which may also
suggest a regulated activity (see D04). Second, the **Articles deny the shareholder any right to
inspect the company's accounting records** [AoA line 543] — a provision a bank may read as
unusual for a wholly-owned subsidiary, and which is against Wiese Advisory's own interest.
